How I think

What design
really is to me

01

A good eye.

Design starts with seeing, really seeing, what works and what doesn't. Before any tool, before any brief.

02

The right tools.

Knowing which instrument serves the moment. Not the trendiest stack, the sharpest one for the job.

03

A commitment to learn.

The craft changes. Curiosity is the only constant. Staying sharp means staying humble.

04

Deeply interested in the why.

Understanding what we're building and why it matters is not optional. It's the foundation everything else rests on.

05

Listening to who uses it.

The user is always the source of truth. Real listening, not assumption, is what separates good design from the rest.

06

Caring for the output.

Every pixel, every word, every interaction. Details are not small, they are the experience.

07

Caring for the people.

Design is an act of service. Who are we talking to? What do they feel? What do they actually need?

08

Caring for how it changes the world.

Good design transforms. It makes things clearer, kinder, more humane. That responsibility never leaves the room.
